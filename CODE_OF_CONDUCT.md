# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Credit prior work and correct overclaims quickly.
- Respect privacy, coordinated disclosure, device ownership, and safety boundaries.
- Focus disagreement on claims, methods, and evidence rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, sexualized attention, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information or device identifiers.
- Pressuring anyone to run a risky device operation.
- Knowingly posting malware, credential material, copyrighted firmware, unsafe payloads, or fabricated evidence.
- Sustained disruption, impersonation, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or lock contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. They will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, available under CC BY 4.0.
