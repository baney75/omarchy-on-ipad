The phrase “Omarchy on iPad” is used descriptively to identify the upstream operating system that this research is attempting to port. It is not presented as this project's own trademark. The site therefore:

- links to and credits the official Omarchy project;
- uses an original project mark instead of Omarchy's logo;

Trademark database screening is only a first check, not legal clearance. The public title should receive attorney review before a commercial launch, fundraising campaign, merchandise, or formal organization. If a rights holder requests a naming or attribution correction, maintainers will evaluate it promptly.
